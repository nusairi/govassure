Security Architect Corner

Document trail

Which security documents to start, update, finish and review in each phase of a service, and what you should have by each gate.

Most documents are not written once. You start them early with what you know, improve them as the design becomes real, and keep them up to date while the service runs. The table shows where each document is in each phase. Each document links to a fill-in template or a community tool.

Use it as a guide, not a rule. A small, low-risk service may need less; a high-risk one may need more. Keep the classification of your documents generic here, and follow your organisation's own handling rules.

Documents by phase

What the tags mean

New
Start the document.
Update
Bring it up to date with what you now know.
Final
Finish it: it should be complete and agreed.
Review
Check it is still right, and change it if not.
Use
Use it to do the work or make a decision.
Close
Close it off and record the outcome.

What you should have by each gate

Before a service moves on, check you have these. This is the same information as the table, set out by gate. The lifecycle map has the gate questions for each phase.

Before moving to alpha (end of discovery)

  • Data and sensitivity summary: a first data and sensitivity summary.
  • Risk register: a first risk register.
  • Risk owners and sign-off: named risk owners (by role) and how sign-off works.
  • Threat model: an early sketch of the threats.
  • Decision records: decision records for any decision that affects security.
  • Supplier assurance: an early check of the likely suppliers.

Before moving to beta (end of alpha)

  • Data and sensitivity summary: an updated data and sensitivity summary.
  • Risk register: an updated risk register with owners.
  • Risk owners and sign-off: updated risk owners and decisions.
  • Threat model: a threat model the team understands.
  • Security design: a first security design with a recommended approach.
  • Decision records: decision records for any decision that affects security.
  • Supplier assurance: supplier assurance questions sent and answers reviewed.
  • Secure by Design evidence: Secure by Design evidence started for the controls in scope.

Before going live (end of beta)

  • Data and sensitivity summary: a final data and sensitivity summary.
  • Risk register: an updated risk register, with every risk treated or accepted.
  • Risk owners and sign-off: the go-live sign-off record.
  • Threat model: a threat model updated for what was built.
  • Security design: a detailed security design and the controls it relies on.
  • Decision records: decision records for any decision that affects security.
  • Supplier assurance: final supplier assurance for every main supplier.
  • Secure by Design evidence: complete Secure by Design evidence.
  • Penetration test scope and results: a penetration test scope, results and a fix plan.
  • Ready for beta checklist: a completed ready for beta checklist.
  • Live security plan: a draft live security plan.

At each live review

  • Data and sensitivity summary: a reviewed data and sensitivity summary.
  • Risk register: a risk register that is kept up to date.
  • Risk owners and sign-off: risk owners checked after any change of role.
  • Threat model: a threat model reviewed for each significant change.
  • Security design: a security design that matches what is running.
  • Decision records: decision records for any decision that affects security.
  • Supplier assurance: supplier assurance reviewed every year.
  • Secure by Design evidence: Secure by Design evidence kept up to date.
  • Penetration test scope and results: a penetration re-test on the agreed cycle.
  • Live security plan: a live security plan in use, with reviews on time.
  • Decommission and disposal plan: a draft decommission and disposal plan.

Before the service is closed

  • Data and sensitivity summary: the data and sensitivity summary, used to plan disposal.
  • Risk register: a closed risk register, with remaining risks accepted.
  • Risk owners and sign-off: the retirement sign-off record.
  • Decision records: decision records for any decision that affects security.
  • Supplier assurance: supplier exit completed, with evidence that data has gone.
  • Decommission and disposal plan: a completed decommission and disposal plan, with evidence.
