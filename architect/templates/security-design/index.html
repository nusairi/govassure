One-page security design

A short summary of how a service is kept secure: the data it holds, how it is built, the main controls and the risks that remain. Start it in alpha and keep it up to date in beta and live.

Your answers stay in this browser. They save automatically to this device only and are never sent to GOV Assured or anyone else. You do not need an account.

Do not enter sensitive or classified details here. Download or print a copy and keep it in your organisation's approved systems.

What good looks like

  • It fits on one or two pages and someone outside the team can understand it.
  • It describes data and sensitivity at a high level, without real records or detailed handling rules.
  • Every control says what it protects, not just which product is used.
  • It names a risk owner by role and says when it was last reviewed.

Short worked example: For a staff meeting-room booking service: “Staff sign in with single sign-on and multi-factor authentication. Only the facilities team has the admin role. All traffic uses TLS and the database is encrypted at rest.”
