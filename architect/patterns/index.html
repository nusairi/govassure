Security Architect Corner

Patterns library

Reusable security designs for problems that come up again and again. Each pattern explains when to use it, shows the parts in a simple diagram, and lists the main risks, the usual controls and the guidance it maps to.

The patterns are vendor-neutral. Adapt them to your organisation's approved platforms and its own handling rules for sensitive information.

Choose a pattern

  • Zero trust access

    Give people access to each application based on who they are and the health of their device, not where they are.

  • Secure cloud landing zone

    Set up cloud accounts, guardrails and shared services before the first workload arrives.

  • Secrets and key management

    Keep passwords, API keys, certificates and encryption keys out of code and under control.

  • Logging to a SIEM

    Collect the right security logs in one protected place, and make sure someone acts on the alerts.

  • Safe third-party integration

    Connect your service to a supplier's system without giving them more access or data than they need.
