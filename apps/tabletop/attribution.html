Version reconciliation

CTID publishes mappings against ATT&CK 16.1; the technique cards are built from ATT&CK v19.1. data/build/extract.py reconciles the two and reports how many mapping edges point at techniques retired or restructured since 16.1. Retired edges are excluded from play and the count is printed on every build, so drift between releases is visible rather than silent.
