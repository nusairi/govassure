Protection matrix

What we use to protect what, layer by layer. Each row is one ring of the OSI Security Rings: the seven OSI layers, plus Layer 0 (hardware and firmware) and Layer 8 (people and governance). Fill in the assets, threats, controls, owner, gaps and how confident you are.

The entries start as generic examples, each marked “Example:”. Replace them with your own, or clear them to start blank. This is a community tool, not official NCSC guidance.
