# Security policy

GOV Assured is a community. This policy explains how to report a security problem in our open-source tools and on the sites we run.

## How to report

Please report the issue privately with GitHub private vulnerability reporting:

https://github.com/nusairi/govassure/security/advisories/new

Describe what the issue is, where it is, and what it could affect. Include enough detail for us to understand and fix it.

Please do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability.

## Scope

This policy covers:

- The GOV Assured open-source tools in this repository
- The sites on govassured.com and its subdomains

The following are out of scope:

- Third-party services and products we link to or rely on
- Social engineering
- Denial-of-service or other volumetric testing
- Automated scanner results with no demonstrated issue

## What we will do

- We will acknowledge your report within 5 working days
- We will aim to fix valid issues and publish a security advisory, with a CVE via GitHub where that is appropriate
- We will credit you in the advisory if you would like us to

## Safe harbour

If you research a security issue in good faith and follow this policy, GOV Assured will not pursue a complaint or legal action against you for that research.
