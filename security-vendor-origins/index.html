How to read this page

  • Documented facts only. Each entry records what a company filing, regulator or reputable report says, with a source. A tag is not a judgement about whether a vendor is trustworthy or risky.
  • Three different countries. Origin is where the company was founded. HQ is where it is headquartered now. Owner is the country of the ultimate owner or a named parent or investor. A company founded in one country can be headquartered and owned elsewhere.
  • Unverified means the fact could not be confirmed from a source, so it has not been guessed.
  • State-linked investor “no” means none was found in the sources checked. It is not proof that none exists, and it does not cover investors inside private-equity funds.
  • Government actions are listings, sanctions, bans or official advice that name the company. An action against a parent company is not copied to its subsidiaries unless the source names them.
  • Where sources disagree, both positions are recorded (for example Huawei’s description of its own ownership and the US Department of Defense’s designation).
  • No individuals. Founder background is recorded only as a company fact, such as “founded in Israel”. Nobody’s nationality or ethnicity is recorded.
  • Links. Official government sources open directly, in a new tab. Other sources are shown as reference codes (R1 onwards). Each code is a host and path in Sources at the foot of this page, not a link to another website.
  • Last verified 27 November 2026. Ownership changes quickly. Pending deals are shown as pending.

    Caveats

    • This is a research reference, not procurement advice. Check current ownership and any government guidance that applies to you before relying on it.
    • Pending deals (CrowdStrike–Zscaler, Proofpoint–Varonis) are shown as pending. Ownership is recorded as it stands until they close.
    • Some shareholdings for China-listed companies come from market-data aggregators and are labelled for confirmation against company filings.
    • EU and UK sanctions lists were not checked systematically. Recorded actions are mainly US listings and UK guidance.
    • No Iranian-founded vendor with a significant UK, EU or US market presence was found, so none is listed.
    • Some non-government sources block automated checks. They were confirmed through other means where noted, and are listed in Sources.
    • Spotted an error? Tell us through the GOV Assured LinkedIn page (opens in a new tab) and include a source.
