About

GOV Assured is a community, built for all of us. Together we solve the problems we all face: cyber security, resilience and assurance. Continuity plans, assessments and checklists are done right in the forms we share, so the work stays in one place and the problems get solved.

This is not a government website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCSC, the Cabinet Office or any UK government department.
